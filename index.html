How I work

Steady steps, shared visibility.

I use a simple, repeatable process to help work move forward accurately and on time.

01

Understand

Read the brief and source files, confirm what’s needed, and note any gaps early.

02

Organise

Track tasks, owners, files, feedback and deadlines so the current status is easy to see.

03

Check

Review completeness, accuracy, consistency, formatting and any required links or fields.

04

Communicate

Record feedback, resolve what I can and promptly raise questions or risks with the right colleague.

What guides me: Follow the agreed process, keep a clear record, and ask when something is uncertain.
